# DLD: what the client needs from the editing system

Client-side reconnaissance, 2026-11-12. One question only: what does DLD (a two-person landscape-architecture
studio) need to be able to change on its new website, how often, who does it, and what has it been promised?

This file says nothing about platforms and was written without looking at any. Where DLD needs something, it is
stated plainly, whether or not it is easy to build.

**Public file.** Prices, payment terms, contact details and private wording are left out on purpose. Emails and
meeting notes are paraphrased and cited by number. Offer wording is given in our English translation.

## Summary

- DLD was sold self-service editing of **news, projects, staff and basic site information**, with images, captions,
  video by link, drafts, a walkthrough and a handbook (Offer p. 4).
- Emails since then added three promises: DLD will **choose the home slideshow photos**, **swap images in projects,
  news and staff**, and **add each year's Christmas card** themselves (#26, #35).
- Emails also narrowed one thing: **page texts are fixed** (front page, Þjónusta, Um okkur). DLD has accepted that
  in practice. The matching answer on **images** (everything else is fixed) went out on 2026-11-12 and has no reply
  yet.
- Two people will edit. One writes Icelandic and drafts text in Word; the other corresponds in English and is the
  one asking to learn uploading. **The language of the admin and the handbook has never been discussed.**
- History: about 40 projects and 14 news posts in 2011–2019, added in small batches a few times a year. **Nothing
  has been added since December 2019.** DLD says it has a lot of new material waiting.
- Expect a catch-up burst after launch, then long gaps between editing sessions (inferred). People who edit four
  times a year forget how. Clear labels and the handbook matter more than features.
- The first hard date is **December 2026**: the Christmas card, promised as something DLD will post themselves.
- After launch the old site is switched off. From then on the new system holds the only working copy of DLD's
  content, and DLD holds no account of its own anywhere in the setup (inferred).
